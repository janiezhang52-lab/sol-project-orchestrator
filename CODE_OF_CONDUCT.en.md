# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | English

Be kind, specific, and respectful of facts and boundaries. People with different experience and viewpoints are welcome. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

Keep discussion focused on behavior, evidence, and reproducible results. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove clearly inappropriate content and restrict people who repeatedly disrupt collaboration. Report serious concerns through a private channel already publicly confirmed by the maintainer; do not spread sensitive information in public discussion.
